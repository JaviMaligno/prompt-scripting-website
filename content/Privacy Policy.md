## 2. Information We Collect

## 3. How We Use Your Data, and Our Lawful Basis

We process personal data only for the purposes below. Each is listed with the lawful basis under Article 6 of the UK GDPR that we rely on.

- **Providing the Service** — creating your account, signing you in, and letting you create, store, run, export and delete your templates and datasets. *Basis: performance of a contract with you.*
    
- **Running a Pro subscription** — knowing whether your account is entitled to Pro, and acting on what Stripe tells us about your subscription. *Basis: performance of a contract with you.*
    
- **Keeping the Service working and safe** — debugging, abuse monitoring, safety filtering, and preventing misuse of the Service or of the AI platforms it works with. *Basis: our legitimate interests in running a service that is not broken and is not abused, weighed against your interests.*
    
- **Writing to you about Prompt Scripter**, where you gave us your address through the sign-up form on our website. *Basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.*
    
- **Meeting legal obligations** — including keeping records the law requires us to keep, and responding to lawful requests. *Basis: compliance with a legal obligation.*
    

We do **not** sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

## 4. Automated Decision-Making

We do not make decisions about you by automated means that produce legal effects or similarly significant effects. Enforcing a plan limit — declining to create an eleventh template on the free plan, say — is a fixed rule applied to a count, not a decision about you.

## 5. Who Receives Your Data

We keep this list as short as running the Service allows.

- **Stripe** — Stripe takes the payment and is the merchant of record for the sale, which makes Stripe the seller and a controller in its own right for the purchase, the receipt and the tax on it. When you pay, Stripe collects and holds your payment and billing details under its own privacy policy. What comes back to us is your subscription status and an identifier, not your card.
    
- **Our infrastructure suppliers** — the hosting platform and the managed database that run the Service, which necessarily hold what you store in it. They act on our instructions as processors. We will tell you who they currently are if you ask.
    
- **Third-party AI platforms** — see section 9. These are the platforms you are already using; Prompt Scripter works alongside them.
    
- **Anyone we are legally required to disclose to** — a court, a regulator, or law enforcement acting under a valid legal power.
    

We do not pass your data to anyone else.

- Stored chat data is kept only as long as needed for you to use the Service.
    
- If you delete data, it will be removed from your view immediately.
    
- We may retain deleted data briefly (within third-party AI providers’ retention policies, e.g. 24h–55 days) for safety and legal purposes.
    
- An email address submitted through the website sign-up form is kept until you ask us
  to remove it, which you can do at any time by writing to **info@javieraguilar.ai**.
  We use it only to send occasional updates about Prompt Scripter. We do not sell it,
  and we do not pass it to anyone else for their own marketing.

If you close your account, we delete your account data and the content you stored, subject to the short windows above and to any record we must keep for tax or legal reasons.
    
- The record of a payment is kept by Stripe as the seller, under Stripe’s own retention rules, and by us where UK tax law requires it.

You can exercise these rights by contacting us at **info@javieraguilar.ai**. We will respond within one month.

## 8. Security

- Traffic between the extension, our website and our backend is encrypted in transit over HTTPS.
    
- Passwords are stored hashed, never in a form we can read.
    
- Sessions use tokens that expire.
    
- We never handle your card details; Stripe does.
    
- Access to the systems holding user data is limited to the person operating the Service, and is not given to anyone who does not need it.
    

No service can promise data is safe against every possible attack, and we do not make that promise. If a breach affects your data and the law requires us to tell you, we will.
